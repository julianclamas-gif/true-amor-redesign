Princess True Amor

Welcome to True Amor Foundation

Honoring hearts. Inspiring hope. Supporting families.

True Amor Foundation was created out of love — inspired by the life and legacy of our precious Princess True. We work to ensure no child suffers or loses their life to an undetected or untreated heart condition.

Through partnerships, education, and advocacy, we strengthen pediatric care and uplift families through community empowerment — with compassion, hope, advocacy, integrity, collaboration, and legacy at the heart of everything we do.

Our vision

“Not every happy life beats with a healthy heart.”

A future where every child, regardless of circumstance, has a healthy heart and a chance to thrive — locally and globally.

Our core values

Guided by love. Driven by purpose.

At the heart of True Amor Foundation are values that reflect compassion, hope, and integrity — bringing healing and lasting impact to families.

Compassion

We serve families with empathy, love, and understanding during their most challenging times.

Hope

We believe in the power of hope to heal hearts and inspire change.

Advocacy

We raise awareness and stand for children affected by heart disease, ensuring their needs are heard.

Integrity

We operate with transparency, accountability, and respect in everything we do.

Collaboration

We partner with families, medical professionals, and communities to maximize impact.

Legacy

We honor the memory of every child who has fought bravely by continuing to spread love and awareness.
